About This Site

What is De-Googling?

De-Googling is the process of reducing or eliminating your reliance on Google products and other Big Tech services that harvest your personal information. Every search you make, every email you send, and every video you watch is tracked, analysed, and sold to advertisers.

This site breaks down why your privacy matters, which companies are collecting your data, and what you can actually do about it. From switching your search engine to replacing your entire operating system, every step counts.
